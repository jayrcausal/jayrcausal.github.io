About me

I am a graduate student of MIT EECS joint Mathematics and core member of Eric and Wendy Schmidt Center at Broad Institute of Harvard and MIT. I am an affiliated researcher closely working with Prof. Biwei Huang(UC San Diego). Prior to leaving New York, I was fortunately supervised by Prof.Bryant Moy at NYU Center for Data Science and Michele Santacatterina at Grossman School of Medicine at NYU Langone. I received my master degree jointly offered by School of Engineering and Courant Institute of Mathematics and Bachelor in Civil Engineering from BJTU.

My long-term research interests are geometry principles driving the underly biology through the lens of algebraic structure.

  • Algebro-geometric principles in machine learning: I use algebraic geometry to formulate theory-driven frameworks for machine learning, viewing learning models as geometric objects and studying representation,equivalence, and identifiability as intrinsic structural properties.
  • AI for Biology: Building on these frameworks, I am interested in translating algebro-geometric learning theory into practical tools for biological discovery, in particular causality, next-generation precise gene-editing and cell state control

Location: Charles River, Cambridge,Massachusetts, United States.
