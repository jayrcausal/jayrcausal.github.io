About Me

I am a graduate student and an affiliated researcher working closely with Prof. Biwei Huang (UC San Diego). I received my master’s degree jointly offered by NYU Tandon and Courant Institute of Mathematics, where I had fortunate to be advised by Prof. Bryant Moy and Michele Santacatterina.

My long-term research interests lie in developing geometry-driven theoretical frameworks for next-generation machine learning, and in applying these principles to drive biological discovery.

Algebro-Geometric Principles in Machine Learning

Formulating theory-driven frameworks, viewing learning models as geometric objects, and studying representation, equivalence, and identifiability as intrinsic structural properties.

AI for Biology

Translating algebraic-geometric learning theory into practical tools for biological discovery, including causality analysis, precise gene editing, and control of cell states.
