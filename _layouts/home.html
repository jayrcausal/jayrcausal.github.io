About Me

I am a graduate student of MIT EECS joint Mathematics and core member of Eric and Wendy Schmidt Center at Broad Institute of Harvard and MIT. I am an affiliated researcher working closely with Prof. Biwei Huang (UC San Diego). I received my master’s degree jointly offered by NYU Tandon and Courant Institute of Mathematics, where I had fortunate to be advised by Prof. Bryant Moy and Michele Santacatterina.

My long-term research interests lie in understanding biology through geometric principles and algebraic structures.

Algebro-Geometric Principles in Machine Learning

Formulating theory-driven frameworks, viewing learning models as geometric objects, and studying representation, equivalence, and identifiability as intrinsic structural properties. This theoretical foundation helps uncover deep structural properties of machine learning systems.

AI for Biology

Translating algebraic-geometric learning theory into practical tools for biological discovery, including causality analysis, precise gene editing, and control of cell states. The focus is on connecting rigorous theory with impactful biological applications.
