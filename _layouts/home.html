About Me

I am a graduate student of MIT EECS joint Mathematics and core member of Eric and Wendy Schmidt Center at Broad Institute. I work closely with Prof. Biwei Huang (UC San Diego). Previously, I was supervised by Prof. Bryant Moy and Michele Santacatterina at NYU. I hold a joint master’s degree from NYU Tandon and Courant.

My long-term research interests are in the geometry principles underlying biology through the lens of algebraic structure.

  • Algebro-geometric principles in machine learning: Formulating theory-driven frameworks, studying learning models as geometric objects, and analyzing representation, equivalence, and identifiability as intrinsic structural properties.
  • AI for Biology: Translating algebro-geometric theory into practical tools for biological discovery, including causality, precise gene editing, and cell state control.
