About Me

I am a graduate student of MIT EECS joint Mathematics and core member of Eric and Wendy Schmidt Center at Broad Institute of Harvard and MIT. I am an affiliated researcher working closely with Prof. Biwei Huang (UC San Diego). I received my master’s degree jointly offered by Tandon Engineering and Courant Institute of Mathematics from New York University, whereI had fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Bryant Moy and Michele Santacatterina at NYU.

My long-term research interests lie in understanding biology through geometric principles and algebraic structures.

  • Algebro-geometric principles in machine learning:
    Formulating theory-driven frameworks, studying learning models as geometric objects, and analyzing representation, equivalence, and identifiability as structural properties.
  • AI for Biology:
    Translating theory into practical tools for biological discovery, including causality, precise gene editing, and cell state control.
